Welcome to Smart India Hackathon

Harnessing the immense technological talent of youth across higher educational institutions.

Smart India Hackathon is a joint initiative launched by the Ministry of Education (MoE), All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), and Persistent Systems.
It has proven to be the world’s largest open innovation model, inviting bright technological minds from universities, engineering colleges, and technical institutes to create transformative software and hardware solutions.


Every year, central government ministries, state departments, public sector units, and premier private industries post their real-world critical operational challenges as official problem statements.
Under the mentorship of industry veterans and academic experts, student teams brainstorm, architect, code, and deploy functional prototypes over a rigorous 36-hour non-stop grand finale.

Dual Pillars of Smart India Hackathon

Addressing both digital algorithmic excellence and physical hardware product design.

💻

Software Edition

Focuses on full-stack web applications, native mobile solutions, machine learning algorithms, cybersecurity frameworks, blockchain ledgers, and cloud infrastructures designed to resolve national workflow bottlenecks.


Duration: 36 Hours Continuous Coding
⚙️

Hardware Edition

Challenges student engineers to design IoT devices, robotics, smart sensor telemetry, drone automation, embedded circuits, and renewable power prototypes addressing defense, healthcare, and rural infrastructure.


Duration: 5 Days Intensive Fabrication
