About Smart India Hackathon

A flagship initiative uniting government, academia, and industry under a single banner.

Smart India Hackathon (SIH) is the world’s largest hackathon and open innovation model.
Conceived in 2017 under the visionary leadership of the Government of India, SIH provides students with an unprecedented opportunity to solve genuine, pressing operational hurdles faced by ministries, armed forces, public infrastructure departments, and premier corporate entities.


Instead of speculative classroom problem solving, participants engage directly with curated challenge statements.
Selected finalists travel to designated Nodal Centers established in premier technological institutes across all states, participating in a high-octane 36-hour non-stop competition evaluated by senior government scientists and industry chief technical officers.

Vision and Core Objectives

Driving India towards technological self-reliance and grassroots innovation.

🎯

National Problem Solving

To harness the intellectual energy of over 100,000 engineering and polytechnic students to create direct digital solutions for government ministries and rural communities.


Focus: Healthcare, Agriculture, Defense & Clean Energy
🚀

Startup Incubation

Winning prototypes receive direct patent support, seed grants, and incubation fast-tracking at government Atal Incubation Centers (AICs) and college innovation cells.


Focus: Commercialization & Enterprise Growth
🤝

Inclusive Team Collaboration

Every team consists of multi-disciplinary engineers, designers, and domain analysts, ensuring balanced technical architectures and inclusive leadership.


Mandate: At least 1 female team member in every team

Organizing Leadership & Stakeholders

Supported by premier public ministries and national technology councils.

🏛️ Governing Bodies (Formatted Font & Semantic Text)

1. Ministry of Education (MoE): Apex government body championing national education policy and youth technological empowerment.

2. All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE): Statutory authority standardizing and elevating technical education curricula.

3. Ministry of Education's Innovation Cell (MIC): Spearheads institution-level innovation councils and patent support.

4. Persistent Systems: Founding industrial technology partner providing technological infrastructure and mentorship panels.
