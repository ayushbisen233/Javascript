Flagship Breakthrough Innovations

Real examples of student code making a tangible difference to citizen services.

🛰️

Geo-Tagging for Water Bodies

Beneficiary: Ministry of Jal Shakti
A computer vision satellite telemetry tool to detect encroachments and measure freshwater reservoir capacities in real-time.


Impact: Deployed across 14 states
🚆

AI Railway Track Fault Detection

Beneficiary: Ministry of Railways
An edge-AI hardware camera mounted beneath locomotives detecting micro-fissures and alignment gaps at speeds up to 100 km/h.


Impact: Commercialized by Indian Railways partner
🌾

Smart Soil Nutrients Sensor

Beneficiary: Ministry of Agriculture
A low-cost optical IoT probe that tests soil nitrogen, phosphorus, and potassium (NPK) in under 60 seconds with mobile sync.


Impact: 2 Patents granted to college inventors
