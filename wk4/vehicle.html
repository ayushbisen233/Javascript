Vehicle Registration Validator

Enter your Vehicle Registration Number:

Format: SS DD AA NNNN (e.g., MH12AB1234)

Validation Rules:

  • Chars 1-2: Uppercase letters (State Code)
  • Chars 3-4: Digits (District Code)
  • Chars 5-6: Uppercase letters (Series)
  • Chars 7-10: Digits (Vehicle Number)
  • Total length must be exactly 10 characters
